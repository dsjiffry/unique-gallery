# Unique Gallery

A fast, private photo gallery for Windows with a slideshow that **never repeats an image until it has shown them all**, even after you close and reopen it.

<a href="https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PHVQWLN7F06"><img src="https://get.microsoft.com/images/en-us%20dark.svg" alt="Get it from Microsoft" width="200"/></a>

**[Get Unique Gallery from the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PHVQWLN7F06)** · available from 19 November 2026

## Features

- **Unique slideshow:** random order (or your sort order), and no image repeats until every image in the folder has been shown. It remembers where it left off between sessions, and new photos join the rotation automatically.
- **Handles big folders:** smooth with tens of thousands of images.
- **HDR support:** real HDR on HDR displays, and tone-mapped HDR on standard screens.
- **Viewer:** fullscreen, keyboard, mouse-wheel and back/forward-button navigation, with an adjustable slideshow interval.
- **Private by design:** no accounts, no internet, no telemetry. Slideshow history and the optional thumbnail cache stay in a hidden folder next to your photos.

## Privacy

Unique Gallery doesn't collect or share any personal data. Read the full [privacy policy](privacy.md).
