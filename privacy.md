# Unique Gallery: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Unique Gallery doesn't collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics,
no ads and no telemetry, and it never connects to the internet.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Slideshow history and optional thumbnail cache.** These are stored inside the folder you open, in a
  hidden subfolder named `.unique-gallery`. It's excluded from Windows Search indexing. It stays with your
  photos, and deleting it removes all of the app's data for that folder. You can turn thumbnail caching
  off and delete the cache from the app's **⋯** menu.
- **App preferences** (slideshow interval, sort order, subfolder option) are kept in the app's private
  local storage. They contain no file names or folder paths.
- If a folder is read-only, its history is kept in the app's private local storage under an anonymised
  (hashed) name instead.

Uninstalling the app removes its private local storage. The `.unique-gallery` folders inside your photo
folders stay until you delete them.
